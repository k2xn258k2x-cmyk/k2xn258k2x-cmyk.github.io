Reading a group table properly
Points decide the order first, and after that the tie-breaks matter more than most viewers realise. Goal difference across the group usually separates level teams, then goals scored, then the result of the match between the tied sides. In an expanded format the third place finishers are ranked against each other across all twelve groups, which means a team can be knocked out by a result in a group it never played in. That is why the final round of group matches is scheduled simultaneously.

Bracket

Work forward from the round of thirty two. Type a name into a slot and it carries into the next round automatically, so you can see the path to the final take shape as you go.

The bracket is fixed before a ball is kicked, which means the half of the draw a team lands in matters as much as its group. Fill in the round of thirty two first and you can see immediately which two contenders are on a collision course three rounds early.

What the overconfidence number means
It compares how sure you said you were against how often you were right. If your very sure calls land at roughly the same rate as your coin flips, your confidence is not carrying information and you are reading the tournament through hope rather than evidence. Well calibrated predictions show a clear gap between the three confidence bands. This is the single most useful thing a prediction log can tell you, and almost nobody tracks it.

Host cities

Sixteen cities across three countries, spread over four time zones and climates that range from desert heat to Pacific coast mild. If you are travelling, the distances between them are the thing people underestimate most.

CityCountryConditionsWorth knowing
Planning travel between them
Treat this as three separate countries rather than one host, because that is what it is. Crossing a border means a passport check and possibly a visa depending on your nationality, and the flight between the furthest apart host cities is longer than many intercontinental hops. Following one team through a group stage can mean three cities in eight days, so read the fixture locations before booking anything. Fans who plan around a single region rather than a single team almost always have the better trip.
