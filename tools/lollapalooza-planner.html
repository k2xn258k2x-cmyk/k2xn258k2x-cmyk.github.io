How to resolve a clash

Rank honestly before the day rather than in the moment, because in the moment you will follow whoever your group is walking toward. When two sets you rated unmissable overlap, the usual answer is to take the first half of one and walk during the changeover, accepting that you will arrive at the back of the second crowd. Do not plan back to back sets on stages at opposite ends of the site. Crossing a large festival footprint through a moving crowd takes far longer than the map suggests, and the planner counts your stage changes for exactly this reason.

Where budgets actually break

Three lines account for most overspend. On site food and drink is the first, because prices inside a festival footprint bear no relation to prices outside it and four days of them compound quickly. Transport is the second, since late night surge pricing arrives exactly when the whole site empties at once and everyone reaches for the same app. The third is the one nobody plans for: the replacement purchase. A lost charger, a soaked jacket, a phone battery bought at a stall for three times its worth. Add a contingency line and treat it as spent rather than as savings.

Check the rules before you pack

Bag size limits, what counts as a permitted container, whether reusable bottles are allowed empty, and re-entry policy all vary by festival and change between editions. Everything on this list is chosen to be broadly sensible, but the official policy page for your event is the authority and it is worth reading the week before rather than at the gate.

Heat and crowds

The two things most likely to end a festival day early, and both are largely preventable. Read this once before the weekend rather than looking it up when someone is already in trouble.

This is general preparation guidance, not medical advice. If someone is confused, has stopped sweating, faints, or does not recover quickly in shade with water, find festival medical staff immediately rather than waiting to see whether it improves. Every large festival has medical tents, and using them early is the correct call.

Group plan

Phones die, signal collapses when eighty thousand people try to post at once, and groups split within the first hour. The fix is agreeing in advance what happens when that occurs, and writing it somewhere that is not a group chat you cannot load.
