Chapter map

Every chapter broken into its movements, with the structural job each one does, the motifs that surface, and the question the chapter is built to make you ask. Open a chapter to see its beats.

Theme and motif tracker

Fifteen recurring images and ideas, each mapped chapter by chapter so you can see where a pattern starts, where it peaks, and where it goes quiet. A filled dot means the motif carries weight in that chapter, a solid dot means the chapter is its centre of gravity.

Character map

Nine figures, each with the desire that drives them, the illusion they hold, the cost they pay, and who they are positioned against. Use the contrast pairs when an essay question asks you to compare.

Quotation bank

Passages worth building an argument on, each with its chapter locator, the motifs it serves, and a note on what it can actually prove. Search by keyword, filter by chapter or motif, then send anything useful to My notes.

Essay angles

Twelve arguable positions, each with the counter-reading you have to handle and the evidence trail that supports it. An angle that no reasonable person could dispute is not an argument, so every one of these has an opposing case attached.

My notes

Everything you marked while reading and everything you saved from the quotation bank, gathered in one sheet. Notes live in your browser only. Export before clearing your browsing data.
